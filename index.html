Our Purpose & Mission

A Home for Worship, Education, and Brotherhood

Baitul Jannah Jame Masjid serves as the spiritual home for Muslims in Cheektowaga and the greater Western New York area. Founded on principles of devotion, inclusive community service, and Islamic learning, we strive to enrich the lives of individuals and families.

From five daily prayers and Friday Jummah services to evening Quranic studies for youth and family gatherings, our doors are open to all seeking peace, guidance, and community connection.

Spiritual Elevation

Daily congregational prayers and spiritual remembrance (Dhikr).

Islamic Education

Authentic Quranic recitation, Tajweed, and youth Islamic studies.

What We Offer

Masjid Services

Serving the holistic needs of our community through religious, educational, and social services.

✦ ۞ ✦

Daily & Friday Prayers

Full 5 daily congregational prayers led by qualified Imams, along with two Jummah sessions on Fridays.

  • Clean, climate-controlled prayer hall
  • Dedicated Sisters' prayer gallery
  • Wudu and washroom facilities

Quranic & Islamic Studies

Comprehensive educational programs designed for children, youth, and adults to deepen Islamic knowledge.

  • Hifz (Quran Memorization) Track
  • Tajweed & Arabic Pronunciation
  • Evening & Weekend Youth Classes

Nikah & Marriage Guidance

Official Islamic matrimony services, Nikah certificate issuance, and confidential pre-marital consultation.

  • Officiated Nikah Ceremony
  • Official Islamic Marriage Certificate
  • Family Counseling Support

Janazah (Funeral) Assistance

Compassionate end-of-life support, Janazah prayer organization, and burial assistance for grieving families.

  • Ghusl (Washing) Guidance
  • Janazah Funeral Prayer
  • Cemetery Coordination

Youth & Sisters Circle

Empowering activities, discussion circles, and social events tailored for young Muslims and sisters.

  • Weekly Sisters' Halaqa
  • Youth Leadership Mentorship
  • Recreational & Sports Outings

Zakat & Community Support

Transparent collection and distribution of Zakat ul-Mal, Zakat ul-Fitr, and emergency financial assistance.

  • Direct Local Beneficiary Vetting
  • Zakat Calculation Guidance
  • Needy Family Relief Fund
